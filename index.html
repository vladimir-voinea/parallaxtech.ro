The studio

Small team, engineered properly.

Parallax Technologies is an independent software studio based in Romania. We come from engineering, and it shows in what we make: Breadboard runs a real electrical solver, Scribe keeps your recordings on your own device, Hushwake was built around one promise and keeps it.

  • Private by default. Our apps don't sell data or show ads.
  • Paid once. No subscriptions for the basics, no tricks.
  • Made in-house. Design, code and art, start to finish.
